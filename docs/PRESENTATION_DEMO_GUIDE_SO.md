# Hagaha Bandhigga Nidaamka Canshuurta iyo Hantida Hargeysa

## Xogta gelitaanka

- Cinwaanka: `http://localhost:5173`
- Maamule demo: `demo_admin`
- Furaha: `DemoAdmin!2026_Km9`
- Dhammaan magacyada, taleefannada, iimayllada, guryaha iyo qiimayaasha ku bilaabma `DEMO` waa xog mala-awaal ah oo bandhig jaamacadeed keliya loogu talagalay.

## Socodka bandhigga (8–10 daqiiqo)

### 1. Gal nidaamka — 30 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** Bogga Login, geli magaca iyo furaha kore, kadib guji **Sign into Portal**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Nidaamku wuxuu leeyahay gelitaan ammaan ah iyo kala-xadayn shaqo. Hadda waxaan ku galay awoodda maamulaha.”
- **Xogta demo:** Akoonka `admin`.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Dashboard-ka guud iyo menu-ga dhammaan qaybaha maamulka.

### 2. Sharax Dashboard-ka — 60 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Dashboard**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Tiradani si toos ah ayay uga imanaysaa kaydka xogta; lama qorin si go'an. Waxaan haynaa 20 hanti demo ah oo leh iib, kiro, bannaan, la iibiyey iyo la kiraystay.”
- **Xogta demo:** Ka eeg kaadhadhka Total Properties, Available, Sold iyo Rented; sidoo kale sawirrada sale/rent.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** 20 hanti, 9 available, 5 sold, 6 rented; 12 sale iyo 8 rent kadib seed-ka ugu dambeeya; jaantus bilaha Janaayo–Agoosto.

### 3. Tus milkiilayaasha — 45 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Clients**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Qaybtani waxay kaydisaa milkiilayaasha hantida. Xogta demo oo dhan waa mala-awaal, iimaylladuna waxay isticmaalaan example.com.”
- **Xogta demo:** Raadi `Axmed Maxamed Warsame` ama `demo.owner01@example.com`.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Magac, telefoon demo, iimayl, xaafad iyo tirada hantida milkiilaha.

### 4. Muuji sida macmiil loo daro — 40 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Add Client**; fur foomka laakiin ha kaydin haddii aan loo baahnayn.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Shaqaaluhu wuxuu geliyaa magaca, telefoonka, iimaylka, cinwaanka iyo faahfaahin. Nidaamku wuxuu hubiyaa xogta ka hor kaydinta.”
- **Xogta demo:** Tusaale ahaan isticmaal magaca `TUSAALE Cabdi Nuur` adigoon kaydin.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Foom cad iyo xaqiijinta fields-ka.

### 5. Tus kaydka hantida — 55 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Property Management** ama **Properties**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Hanti kasta waxay ku xidhan tahay milkiile, nooc listing, xaalad, qiime, goob iyo coordinates.”
- **Xogta demo:** Raadi `DEMO-PR-001`; tijaabi filter-ka **sale** iyo **available**.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** `[DEMO-PR-001] Modern Three-Bedroom House – Jigjiga Yar` iyo xogteeda isku xidhan.

### 6. Fur faahfaahinta iyo sawirka — 45 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** Edit/View ee `DEMO-PR-009` ama property kale oo demo ah.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Sharaxaadda waxaa ku jira reference, nooca dhismaha, cabbirka, qolalka iyo taariikhda diiwaangelinta. Sawirku waa tusaale sharci ahaan dib loo isticmaali karo, mana aha guriga dhabta ah.”
- **Xogta demo:** `[DEMO-PR-009] Warehouse – Masalaha`.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Milkiilaha, qiimaha, goobta, GIS coordinates, xaaladda iyo sawirka maxalliga ah.

### 7. Sharax iibka iyo kirada — 40 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Property Management**; beddel filter-ka **sale**, kadib **rent**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Nidaamku wuxuu taageeraa laba listing type: sale iyo rent. Qiimaha rent-ku waa bille; qiimaha sale-ku waa qiimaha iibka/qiimeynta demo.”
- **Xogta demo:** Sale: `DEMO-PR-002`; Rent: `DEMO-PR-003`.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** 12 sale listings iyo 8 rental listings oo xaaladahoodu is waafaqsan yihiin.

### 8. Sharax canshuurta — 55 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Tax Management**, kadib **Generate Bill** ee `DEMO-PR-001`.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Nooca hadda jira wuxuu sameeyaa tax roll laga xisaabiyo qiimaha property-ga iyadoo la adeegsanayo rate-ka demo ee 1%. PDF bill-ka waxaa sameeya backend-ka, ficilkana audit log ayaa qabta.”
- **Xogta demo:** `DEMO-PR-001`, qiime $145,000; canshuurta tusaalaha ahi waa $1,450.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Total assessed value iyo estimated tax levied oo aan eber ahayn, kadib PDF bill.
- **Xusuusin daacad ah:** Noocan mashruucu weli ma laha jadwal gaar ah oo tax assessments/payments ah ama paid/pending/overdue statuses.

### 9. Tus khariidadda GIS — 50 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **GIS Map**; raadi `Masalaha`; beddel status filters.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Dhammaan 20-ka property waxay leeyihiin coordinates qiyaas xaafadeed ah, mana aha cinwaanno guryo gaar ah. Midabku wuxuu muujinayaa available, rented ama sold.”
- **Xogta demo:** `DEMO-PR-009` iyo `DEMO-PR-020` ee Masalaha.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Calaamado ku kala firdhan Hargeysa; cagaar available, oranji rented, cawl sold. Marker-ka guji si popup-ku u muujiyo title, goob, type, status iyo qiime.

### 10. Tus Analytics iyo Reports — 55 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Analytics**, kadib **Reports**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Jaantusyadu waxay ka yimaadaan queries-ka database-ka: status distribution, rent iyo sale, bilaha la abuuray, iyo dakhliga properties-ka sold. Reports-ku waxay soo saaraan CSV, Excel iyo PDF.”
- **Xogta demo:** Status chart-ka 9/5/6 iyo trend-ka Janaayo–Agoosto.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Jaantusyo xog leh iyo badhamada export-ka.

### 11. Tus Users iyo Activity Log — 45 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **User Management**, kadib **Activity Log**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Roles-ka dhabta ah waa admin, agent iyo viewer. Audit log-ku wuxuu muujinayaa hawlgallada demo seeder-ka iyo ficillada caadiga ah sida login ama tax bill generation.”
- **Xogta demo:** `demo_agent` iyo `demo_viewer`; raadi `[DEMO]` gudaha Activity Log.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Akoonnada roles kala duwan iyo taariikh hawleed la fahmi karo.

### 12. Backups, Settings iyo gunaanad — 40 ilbidhiqsi

- **Guji:** **Backups**, kadib **Settings**.
- **Waxaad tidhaahdaa:** “Maamuluhu wuxuu samayn karaa backup/restore, wuxuuna dejin karaa luqad, timezone iyo notifications. Nidaamku wuxuu isku daraa milkiilaha, hantida, GIS, canshuurta tusaalaha, warbixinta iyo audit-ka hal meel.”
- **Xogta demo:** Ha restore-gareyn inta bandhiggu socdo; kaliya muuji controls-ka.
- **Waxa la arki doono:** Qalabka backup-ga iyo dejimaha isticmaalaha.

## Akoonnada demo

| Role | Username | Password |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | `demo_admin` | `DemoAdmin!2026_Km9` |
| Agent | `demo_agent` | `DemoAgent!2026_Aq7` |
| Viewer | `demo_viewer` | `DemoViewer!2026_Zp4` |

Agent-ku wuu arki karaa oo wax ka beddeli karaa clients/properties, laakiin ma laha dhammaan awoodaha admin-ka. Viewer-ku xogta ayuu akhriyi karaa, waxna ma beddeli karo.

## Amarada xogta demo

```powershell
# Ku dar ama cusboonaysii xogta demo; duplicates ma sameeyo
npm.cmd run seed:demo

# Ka saar keliya records-ka uu demo seeder-ku calaamadeeyey
npm.cmd run seed:demo:reset
```
